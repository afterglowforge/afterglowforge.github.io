Neon Holdout Privacy Policy

Developer: Afterglow Forge · Effective date: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Neon Holdout (the "game") on Android. If you have questions, contact us at afterglowforge@gmail.com.

Summary

  • We do not operate our own servers, and the game has no accounts or sign-in.
  • Your progress, coins, skins and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads. Our advertising partners collect some device data to provide them, as described below.
  • Optional purchases are handled by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (for example coins, unlocked skins, best scores and settings) locally on your device. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

Neon Holdout includes optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example to revive once per run or to earn progress toward certain skins). Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) and Unity Ads. Ads are only started after you make a privacy choice in the game, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Ads.

To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, these providers may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device information and app diagnostics;
  • your interactions with ads; and
  • information about in-app purchases made in the game.

If you choose personalised ads, the providers may use this information to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you choose non-personalised ads, the game asks the providers to show only non-personalised ads and not to sell your personal information. The providers' handling of this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which also explains how to access or delete data they hold (for example through the privacy icon shown in Unity ads).

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android device settings.

In-app purchases

The game may offer optional one-time purchases of cosmetic skin collections. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive your payment card details. Google's processing is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to communicate with Google Play.

Children

Neon Holdout is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security and retention

Data sent by our advertising and purchase providers is transmitted over encrypted connections. We keep no personal data ourselves; game data stays on your device until you delete it.

Your choices

  • Change your ad privacy choice in Settings > Privacy & Ads.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.
  • Contact us at afterglowforge@gmail.com with any privacy request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
